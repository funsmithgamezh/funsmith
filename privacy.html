Game progress, settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. This local copy exists whether or not you sign in, and uninstalling the App removes it.

4. Accounts and Sign-In

4.1 Optional accounts

An account is not required to play our Apps. If you create one, it is used only to identify you across devices, to back up and restore your progress, and to deliver purchases you have made. We do not require an account to show ads or to use the core features of any App.

4.2 Sign in with Google / Apple

Where an App offers "Sign in with Google" or "Sign in with Apple", authentication is handled by Google LLC or Apple Inc. respectively, under their own privacy policies. We receive only the limited data listed in Section 3.2. You can revoke our access at any time in your Google Account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in your Apple ID settings (Settings → Apple ID → Sign in with Apple).

4.3 Guest play and linking

If you play as a guest and later sign in, we link the account identifier to your existing local progress so you do not lose it. If you sign in on a second device, we may ask which progress you want to keep if the two differ.

4.4 Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the App (typically Settings → Account → Delete Account), or by emailing huijuli2025@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your account record and your synced progress from our servers. See Section 9.4 for details on what is deleted and what we must retain.

5. Data Storage and Synchronisation

5.1 On-device storage

Your game progress, settings and preferences are primarily stored on your device. This data is not readable by us unless you sign in and enable synchronisation.

5.2 Server-side storage

If you sign in, we store a copy of your game progress and the account data described in Section 3.2 on our servers, so that your progress can be restored if you change devices, reinstall an App, or clear your local data. Server-side storage is provided by cloud infrastructure providers acting on our instructions as data processors; they do not use your data for their own purposes.

5.4 Changes to storage practices

We may change how an App stores data — for example, moving from device-only storage to cloud synchronisation, or adopting a new infrastructure provider. This policy already covers server-side storage, so such a change does not require your renewed consent; we will, however, update this policy and its version number, and where the change materially affects your rights we will give notice as described in Section 15.

6.2 Advertising

Advertising keeps our games free. Our advertising partners may use cookies, mobile advertising identifiers and similar technologies to serve ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and detect invalid traffic. Ads may be personalized based on your activity across apps and websites over time.

We do not use the contents of your account, your email address, or your synced game progress to target advertising, and we do not share your email address with advertising partners.

6.3 Opting out of personalized advertising

You can opt out of personalized advertising, and reset or delete your advertising ID, at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on device)
  • Google ad settings (web): adssettings.google.com

Opting out does not remove ads; it means the ads you see are less relevant to you.

9. Your Rights & Choices

9.1 Rights available to everyone

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you, and provide a copy
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete information we hold about you
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Provide your information in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave

If you have an account, most of this is available directly in the App. Otherwise, email huijuli2025@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request", stating which game the request concerns. We respond within 30 days, or within the period required by your local law. We may ask for information to verify that the request comes from you, but we will never ask for your payment card number, password, or a government identity document.

9.2 California and other US state residents

If you live in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Virginia or another state with a comprehensive privacy law, you also have the right to know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the right to delete it, the right to correct it, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Because we show personalized advertising, some laws treat disclosure of advertising identifiers to our advertising partners as "sharing" or as a "sale" for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out as described in Section 6.3, or by emailing us. We honour opt-out preference signals, including the Global Privacy Control, where technically feasible. Account data, including your email address, is never shared with advertising partners and is not part of any sale or sharing.

9.3 Data portability and access

If you have an account, you can request an export of your account data and synced progress in a machine-readable format. We provide it within 30 days.

9.4 Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App, or by emailing us. When you do:

  • Your account record, email address, display name, and synced game progress are deleted from our servers
  • Your local device data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data
  • We may retain limited records where the law requires it, such as transaction records for tax and accounting, and minimal logs needed to prevent fraud and abuse
  • Deletion is irreversible; progress stored only on our servers cannot be recovered afterwards

Guest progress that was never synced is unaffected by account deletion, because we never held it.

9.5 Deletion without an account

If you never created an account, you can delete all game data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings. Advertising identifiers can be reset or deleted in your device settings at any time.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK, where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the store listing for a particular App indicates that it is directed to children, that App does not serve personalized advertising, does not collect advertising identifiers for advertising purposes, and does not offer account creation.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact huijuli2025@gmail.com and we will delete the account and associated data promptly.

11. Data Retention

  • Account data (identifier, email, display name): retained while your account is active, and deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request.
  • Synced game progress: retained while your account is active, and deleted with the account.
  • Support correspondence: up to 24 months after the enquiry is resolved.
  • Aggregated analytics: kept in aggregate or de-identified form, under our providers' default retention periods.
  • Purchase records: as long as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Local device data: on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Backups: residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period (typically no more than 90 days) before being overwritten.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Data transmitted between the Apps and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS, and account data is stored on infrastructure that encrypts data at rest. Access to server-side data is limited to personnel who need it for the purposes described in this policy.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.
